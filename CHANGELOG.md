# Changelog

## 1.1.0 (2026-10-30)

- The History tab can show only today's games, this week's, or the whole season.
- One Skom's minimap button opens all your Skom's addons when two or more are installed (/skoms).
- Spectated arenas are no longer recorded, and the ones already recorded are removed.
- This Week: 2v2 and 3v3 show your games against the games you need for arena points, like SoloQ.
- This Week no longer empties the bracket columns of your other characters when the reset hour reads differently on a login.

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-27)

First release: a 3.3.5a (Warmane) version of ArenaStats by Kallias-Sulfuron, with weekly arena points, all your characters in one window and Solo Queue support.

- Games are not lost on a /reload or disconnect during the arena, or when the scoreboard is slow to arrive.
- A purge of the data can be undone.
- This Week shows your own games next to the team's (`39/10  you 6/12`), so you see what is missing for arena points.
