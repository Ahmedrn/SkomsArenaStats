# Skom's Arena Stats (WoW 3.3.5a, Warmane)

Every arena you play, recorded. One window shows your week on all your characters: games, wins, rating, honor, and the arena points you will get on reset day.

## Features

- **This Week:** for every character:
  - games, W-L, win rate, rating change and honor earned;
  - the **arena points expected at the next reset**, using Warmane's rules;
  - each team's weekly games (turns green once it pays).
- **Characters:** honor and arena points against their caps, team and personal ratings, last seen.
- **History:** every match with the map, duration, both teams' classes, rating change and MMR. Filter by bracket, rated or skirmish, and enemy name.
- **Solo Queue** recognized as its own bracket.
- **Weekly reset** detected automatically, with a reset day option.

## Commands

- `/as` or the minimap button opens the window.
- `/as weekly`, `/as characters`, `/as history`, `/as options`.
